SIDECAR TIMER

Using Sidecar Timer

What you have: a small desk screen that shows one timer at a time.

What you need: a computer with Chrome or Edge, and a USB cable.

1 Plug it in

Connect the small screen to your computer with the USB cable. The screen lights up and shows a face with the words Waiting for the timer page. That means it is ready.

Use the data USB port on the board, not a charge-only cable. If the screen stays dark, try a different cable before anything else.

2 Open the timer page

On the computer, open the Sidecar Timer web page in Chrome or Edge on a desktop. Other browsers cannot talk to the screen.

Click Connect screen. A small window asks which device to use — pick the one that appeared when you plugged the cable in.

The page never sends anything until you click Connect. You can disconnect at any time.

3 Make your own timers

Under Your timers, type a name, type how many minutes, then press Add timer. Add as many as you like — one for homework, one for reading, one for tidying up.

  • Choose a timer — click its name. The big circle and the small screen both switch to it.
  • Edit a timer — hover over it and click Edit, change the name or minutes, then Save timer.
  • Delete a timer — hover over it and click the ×. Your last timer cannot be deleted.
  • Start — press the green button. Press it again to pause. The ↺ button puts the timer back to the start.

Your timers are saved in the browser on this computer. Nothing is uploaded and no account is needed.

4 Read the little face

While a timer runs, the screen shows the moving eyes, timer name, large countdown, and progress bar. The timer keeps counting if you look away from the computer. When it reaches zero, it is finished.

5 Show the local forecast

Turn on Show weather when idle and choose Bothell, Kenmore, or Woodinville. After 12 seconds without activity, the connected screen shows the forecast. Starting or changing a timer brings the timer view back.

Forecast data is provided by the U.S. National Weather Service. The browser needs an internet connection to refresh it.

6 If the screen is upside down

On the page, under Mount side, choose Left to flip the picture 180°. Choose Right to turn it back. This is handy when the screen sits on the other side of your desk.

7 Pack it away

Close the page or press Disconnect screen, then unplug the cable. Your saved timer list stays in the browser for next time.

Quick fixes

What you seeWhat to try
Screen stays darkTry another USB cable, then unplug and plug it back in.
“Web Serial is unavailable”You are in the wrong browser. Open the page in desktop Chrome or Edge.
No device in the Connect windowThe cable is charge-only, or the screen is not plugged in. Try a data cable.
Screen is upside downSwitch Mount side between Left and Right.
Screen shows the waiting face while a timer runsPress Connect screen again, then press the green start button.
Timer did not change on the screenUnplug and replug the USB cable, then reconnect on the page.
