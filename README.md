# Sidecar Timer

A calm desk companion for focus sessions. Set up timers in the browser, connect
your Sidecar screen over USB, and let the buddy keep time while you work.

**Open the timer:** [mattcreator-sys.github.io/sidecar-timer](https://mattcreator-sys.github.io/sidecar-timer/)

## Using Sidecar

1. Open the timer in desktop Chrome or Edge.
2. Plug the screen into the computer and choose **Connect screen**.
3. Select a timer and press **Start**.
4. Turn on **Show weather when idle** to show the Bothell area forecast after the
   screen has been idle for 12 seconds. Choose Bothell, Kenmore, or Woodinville.

Your timer list stays in the browser on your computer. Sidecar does not require an account.
Weather comes from the [National Weather Service](https://www.weather.gov/).

For connection help, open the [use guide](https://mattcreator-sys.github.io/sidecar-timer/guide.html).
